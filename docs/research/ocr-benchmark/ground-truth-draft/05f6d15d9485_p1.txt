RE:   180 Extension Request from City of Montgomery to Complete Negotiations with Norfolk Southern and Waiver of Fee

City of Montgomery, Alabama Response to Docket No. AB-290 (Sub-No. 278X), Central of Georgia Railroad
Company/Norfolk Southern - Abandonment - in Montgomery County, Alabama

City of Montgomery, Alabama Response to Docket No. AB-55 (Sub-No. 728X), CSX Transportation, Inc. -
Discontinuance of Operating Rights - in Montgomery County, Alabama

Dear Ms. Brown:

This request is filed on behalf of the City of Montgomery, which is interested in the property that an abandonment and
discontinuance of operating rights for a rail line from milepost H411.50 to H413.62 for transportation purposes was filed by
Norfolk Southern (NS)/Central of Georgia Railroad (CGR) and CSX.   We understand that the STB issued a Notice of Intent for
Trail Use or abandonment, which provided for a 180 day period in which the City of Montgomery and NS/CGR can negotiate
an interim trail use/rail banking agreement.

We have been in communication with NS representatives in Montgomery and Virginia to negotiate an interim trail use/rail
banking agreement since the STB action occurred.   In addition, we have initiated and completed the process of getting an
appraisal on the referenced property and any other relevant information needed to make a sound decision.  NS/CGR declined
the initial offer of purchase and has initiated the preparation of their own appraisal but has not completed the appraisal.  Based
on continued negotiations and communications that are ongoing, the City of Montgomery hereby request an extension of the
180 day period by an additional 180 days in order to complete all tasks associated with this  property transaction.  Also, the City
of Montgomery requests a waiver of the fee for filing this extension.

Thank you for your consideration in this request.

If you have any questions please feel free to give me a call at (334) 625-2218.
