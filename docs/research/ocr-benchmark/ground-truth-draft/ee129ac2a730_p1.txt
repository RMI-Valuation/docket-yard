Defendant's Reply to Motion of PPL Montana, LLC
For Extension of Deadline for Petition for Reconsideration

Defendant The Burlington Northern and Santa Fe Railway Company (BNSF) hereby
replies to the August 22, 2002 Motion of PPL Montana, LLC (PPL) For Extension of Deadline
for Petition for Reconsideration.  In its Motion, PPL seeks a 20-day extension of the deadline
established under 49 CFR §1115.3 for filing a petition for reconsideration of the Board's August
20, 2002 decision in this case.  PPL also seeks authorization to file a 40-page petition, double the
page limit set by the Board's rules.  For the reasons set out below, BNSF believes that a brief
extension of time may be appropriate to accommodate Mr. Crowley's schedule, but it does not
believe that a 20-day extension of the deadline is justified or that any expansion of the 20-page
limit on petitions for reconsideration would be appropriate.

stand-alone cost decisions.  The Board's August 20, 2002 decision focused on a single, relatively
narrow issue -- PPL's misuse of cross-subsidies.  While the Board also addressed a few
construction cost issues, it found it unnecessary to rule on numerous other complex and disputed
