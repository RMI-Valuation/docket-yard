RE:    STB Docket No. AB-33 (Sub-No. 262X) – Union Pacific Railroad Company
       Abandonment and Discontinuance of the Elm Industrial Lead in Fulton and
       Peoria Counties, Illinois

Dear Ms. Brown:

        On July 21, 2008, the Illinois Department of Natural Resources filed a request for Public
Use Condition and a request for Interim Trail Use Condition (NITU) for approximately 24.7
miles of railroad between milepost 461.5 near Middle Grove and milepost 486.2 at Molitor
Junction, in Fulton and Peoria Counties.  By Order dated November 13, 2008, the Board granted
the request to implement interim trail use and to permit public use negotiations as specifically set
forth in the Order, for a period of 180 days commencing from November 13, 2008, and ending
on May 12, 2009.  On May 9, 2009, the Department filed a request for a 180 day extension, and
by Order dated May 11, 2009, request was so granted, extending the negotiating period from
May 12, 2009 to November 8, 2009. On November 2, 2009, The Department filed a request for
an additional 180 day extension, and by Order dated November 24, 2009, request was so granted,
thus extending the negotiating period from November 8 to May 10, 2010.  The Department filed
a request for an additional 180 day extension, and by Order dated May 17, 2010, request was so
granted, thus extending the negotiating period from May 10 to November 12, 2010. The
Department filed a request for an additional 180 day extension, and by late Order dated
December 14, 2010, request was so granted, thus extending the negotiating period from
November 12, 2010 until May 13, 2011. The Department filed a request for an additional 180
day extension, and by late Order dated June 17, 2011, was so granted, thus extending the
negotiating period from May 13, 2011 until November 13, 2011.  The Department filed a request
for an additional 180 day extension, and by Order dated November 9, 2011, was so granted, thus
extending the negotiating period from November 13, 2011 until May 11, 2012.
