Dear Secretary Williams:   Re AB 33 (Sub No. 143X)

The Iowa Trails Council is asking for another extension of time in
which to complete negotiations to acquire the Union Pacific
railroad right-of-way Between Thornton and Belmond in Wright,
Franklin and Cerro counties in Iowa, for conversion to a trail.

We are enclosing ten copies of this letter for distribution and are
forwarding a copy to Mr. Mack Shumate, Senior General Attorney
for the Union Pacific at the Railroad's office at 101 N. Wacker
Drive in Chicago, Il., 60606.  It is our understanding that the
railroad will agree to another 180 day period in which to continue
our negotiations.

Thanking you for your cooperation in this matter, we remain
