Southern between Clairton, PA and Bellevue, OH (the transaction to which the notice of
exemption in Finance Docket No. 33388 (Sub-No. 98) pertains), the Joint Petition states:
"W&LE will use its best efforts to permit NS to obtain the trackage rights between Jewett and
Bowerston, reflecting the fact that W&LE's rights over that segment are themselves trackage
rights over the Ohio Central Railroad." Norfolk Southern and W&LE wish to clarify that the
referenced line segment between Jewett and Bowerston, over which W&LE has trackage rights,
is owned by the State of Ohio and is operated by The Columbus & Ohio River Rail Road
Company ("C&OR"), which is an operating affiliate railroad of the Ohio Central Railroad
System. The C&OR is the actual grantor of the W&LE's present trackage rights over that
segment.
