Mr. Geoffrey Mann, Vice President and Treasurer of Laidlaw,
in his Verified Statement, appended as Attachment E, explains in
detail that the financial difficulties experienced by Laidlaw
that led to the decision to curtail the cash funding of Greyhound
are related primarily to businesses other than Greyhound.  They
have arisen principally as a result of developments in Laidlaw's
discontinued health care operations and financial issues
associated with its equity investments in the hazardous waste
industry, specifically, its forty-three percent ownership stake
in Safety-Kleen Corp..
