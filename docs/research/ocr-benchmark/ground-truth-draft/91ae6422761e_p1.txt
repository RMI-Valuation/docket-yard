Abandonment Exemption-in Worcester County, MA

Dear Ms. Brown:

By Notice of Exemption served on November 20, 2008, in the above-entitled
proceeding, the Surface Transportation Board (the "Board") provided that the
abandonment of the CSX Transportation, Inc. ("CSXT") 4.2-mile line of railroad
between milepost QBU 0.0 (Fitchburg) and milepost QBU 4.2 (Leominster), in
Worcester County, MA (the "Line") would become effective on December 20, 2008, and
that until November 20, 2009 CSXT could effect abandonment.  By decision served on
November 19, 2009, the Board granted CSXT an extension of the consummation date
until November 20, 2010.

On November 18, 2010, the Georgetown and High Line Railway Company, LLC
("GHLR") filed a request for a Notice of Interim Trail Use and Abandonment ("NITU")
and a one year period for negotiation.  CSXT agreed to the request.  On December 27,
2010, the Board served a NITU authorizing negotiations between GHLR and CSXT for a
one year period ending December 27, 2011.  In a decision served on December 13, 2011,
the Board extended the NITU negotiating period until December 27, 2012.  In a decision
served on February 13, 2013, the Board extended the NITU negotiating period until
December 27, 2013.  In a decision served on December 27, 2013, the Board extended the
NITU negotiating period until December 27, 2014.  In a decision served on January 9,
2015, the Board extended the NITU negotiating period until December 27, 2015.

GHLR and CSXT have negotiated during the period, but even though they have
made progress, they have not reached a final agreement.  CSXT has not yet consummated
abandonment of the Line. Therefore, GHLR respectfully requests that the Board grant a
one year extension of the negotiation period until December 27, 2016.

Enclosed with the original and 10 copies of this letter is a check for the filing fee
of $450.00.
