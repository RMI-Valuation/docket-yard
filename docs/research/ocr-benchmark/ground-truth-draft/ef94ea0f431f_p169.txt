D.5.6	Navigation Effects

SEA reviewed the following changes in the operations and facilities to estimate potential effects
on navigation:

•	Proposed changes in freight train activity on rail line segments, based on the Applicants'
Operating Plan. These changes could affect the operation of movable bridges on that rail
line segment and thus be subject to U.S. Coast Guard regulations.

•	Proposed new bridges or alterations to existing bridges that would be subject to U.S.
Coast Guard approval and U.S. Army Corps of Engineers approvals for dredge and fill
activities.
