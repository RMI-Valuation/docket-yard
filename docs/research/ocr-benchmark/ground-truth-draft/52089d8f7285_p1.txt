SURFACE TRANSPORTATION BOARD

DECISION

STB Docket No. AB-33 (Sub-No. 107X)

UNION PACIFIC RAILROAD COMPANY--ABANDONMENT
EXEMPTION--IN CERRO GORDO COUNTY, IA

Decided:  February 26, 1997

On February 10, 1997, Union Pacific Railroad Company (UP)
filed a verified notice of exemption under 49 CFR 1152
Subpart F--Exempt Abandonments and Discontinuances of Service and
Trackage Rights to abandon an approximately 3.25-mile line of its
railroad known as the Fort Dodge Branch, from milepost 60.5, near
Flint, to milepost 63.75, near Burchinal, in Cerro Gordo County,
IA.

By facsimile filed February 24, 1997, UP requests withdrawal
of its notice of exemption.  UP states that the proposed
abandonment of the Fort Dodge Branch between Flint and Burchinal
would allow the Iowa Department of Transportation to avoid
construction of an overpass on U.S. Highway 18 near Mason City.
UP further states as a result of a recent meeting in Des Moines,
it appears that there may be issues to be discussed and resolved
regarding this project.  Therefore, the request to withdraw the
notice of exemption will be granted.

It is ordered:

1.  UP's request to withdraw its notice of exemption is
granted.

2.  The proceeding is dismissed.

3.  This decision is effective on its service date.

By the Board, Vernon A. Williams, Secretary.
