Re:   STB Docket No. AB-290 (Sub-No. 240X), Norfolk Southern Railway
      Company - Abandonment Exemption - In Gaston County, NC -
      Request for Extension of Consummation Date Deadline

Dear Mr. Williams:

      Norfolk Southern Railway Company (NSR) previously requested that the Board
grant extensions of time to consummate the abandonment of the rail line that was the
subject of NSR's petition for exemption in this docket.  By decisions served December
1, 2004, April 7, 2005 and November 29, 2005, the Board granted extensions of the
consummation date through today, May 9, 2006.  The reason for the extension
requests was that NSR was negotiating with various parties, including the City of
Gastonia and the North Carolina Railroad Company for disposition of the right-of-way
outside the National Trails System Act procedures.  NSR now realizes that those
extension requests were unnecessary because environmental consultation conditions
remain outstanding that are legal barriers to consummation of the abandonment.

      Therefore, NSR does not request a further extension of the date for
consummation of the abandonment in this proceeding.  In line with the Board's
regulations, NSR will consummate the abandonment within 60 days of the completion
of the environmental consultation conditions or, in the event of changed circumstances,
will ask for a further extension of the consummation date within that time period.  In
order to comply with the request of the City of Gastonia that handling of this matter be
expedited, NSR will complete these consultations as soon as possible but believes that
some of the consultations and state agency reviews may take until well into the summer
to complete.
