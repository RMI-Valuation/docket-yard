. . . CIRY retains an obligation to provide service over this line and it
would have to be relieved of that obligation prior to the Cities' planned
reconfiguration . . .

The Municipalities intend to abide by that statement. Thus, the Municipalities will not remove
that segment until CIRY's authority to operate over that segment has been discontinued in an
effective Board decision. CIRY has informed the Municipalities that CIRY will initiate
proceedings for discontinuance of its rail service over that segment.

PIRY ceased operations on the Kellar Branch shortly after the August 10, 2005 effective
date of the Board's decision granting the Municipalities' application for adverse discontinuance
of PIRY's operations on the Branch. That being the case, an action in state court seeking to eject
PIRY from the Branch is not necessary. Consequently, the segment of the Branch at issue can be
lawfully removed without regard to the absence of a state court order ejecting PIRY from the
Branch.1/
