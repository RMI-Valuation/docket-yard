The Burlington Northern and Santa Fe Railway Company—Operation Exemption—A Line of
Railroad Owned by the City of Los Angeles, CA, Through its Board of Harbor Commissioners

The Burlington Northern and Santa Fe Railway Company (BNSF) has filed a verified notice
of exemption under 49 CFR 1150.31 to operate a rail line owned by the City of Los Angeles, CA,
through its Board of Harbor Commissioners, a noncarrier.  The 0.8-mile rail line involved in this
transaction is located between milepost 17.0 and milepost 17.8, in Los Angeles, and will constitute
part of the Alameda Rail Corridor (Corridor).¹

The exemption became effective on December 30, 1998, but the exempted transaction is not
expected to be consummated until approximately March 2002.²
