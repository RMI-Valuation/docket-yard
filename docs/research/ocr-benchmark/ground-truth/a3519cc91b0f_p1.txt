RE: Buffalo & Pittsburgh Railroad Inc. - Abandonment Exemption- In Erie and
Cattaraugus Counties, New York STB Docket No. AB-369 (Sub-No. 7X)

This request is filed on behalf of the New York State Office of Parks, Recreation and Historic Preservation
(Proponent) to request an extension of the deadline for negotiations with Buffalo & Pittsburgh Railroad, Inc.
regarding the Notice of Interim Trail Use in the above-noted proceeding with respect to the rail line between
Milepost 8.4 in the Town of Orchard Park, Erie County, New York and Milepost 36 in the Town of Ashford,
Cattaraugus County, New York, a total distance of 27.6 miles. The current negotiating period expires on April 13,
2013.

Buffalo & Pittsburgh Railroad, Inc. and the Proponent do not anticipate being able to consummate an agreement
regarding the NITU prior to the expiration of the NITU deadline, but wish to continue negotiations. Accordingly,
the Proponent requests a 180-day extension of the NITU negotiation deadline to October 10, 2013. Buffalo &
Pittsburgh Railroad, Inc. has acknowledged that it will concur with this request and will file a letter of concurrence
once a copy of this letter is filed with the Board.

In addition, the State respectfully requests a waiver of the Board's filing fees in accordance with 49 CFR
1002.2(e)(1).
