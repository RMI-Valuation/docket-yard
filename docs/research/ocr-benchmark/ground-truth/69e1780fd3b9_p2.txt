NORFOLK SOUTHERN'S OPPOSITION
TO CAPITOL MATERIALS' MOTION TO COMPEL DISCOVERY

The motion filed by Capitol Materials on February 26, 2002, asking the Board to
order NS to produce documents in response to Capitol's requests, is based entirely on a
series of mischaracterizations and should be denied. The motion is a transparent and
baseless attempt to further lengthen the procedural schedule beyond the extension
already granted by the Board.

First. The crux of Capitol's assertions is that NS allegedly "has failed and
refused to provide the requested discovery information." Capitol Materials
Memorandum at 5. That claim is false. The fact of the matter is that since being served
with Capitol's document requests, NS has diligently worked at the time-consuming and
labor-intensive task of identifying responsive documents, has produced thousands of
pages of responsive documents, and has made clear that it is working to identify and
produce still more.
