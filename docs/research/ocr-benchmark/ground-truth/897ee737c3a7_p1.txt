Dear Secretary Williams:

We are requesting 90 days additional time in which to complete
negotiations for the acquisition of the soon to be abandoned Union
Pacific Railroad right-of-way in Franklin and Cerro Gordo counties in
Iowa, a stretch of 12.38 miles between the cities of Belmond and
Thornton.

I believe that the Union Pacific Railroad is aware of our continued
effort to obtain funds for the purchase of this land and that we are
confident that we will be able to finalize these negotiations well
within the time period requested.

We are forwarding a copy of this letter to the Omaha and Chicago
offices of the Union Pacific Railroad and are sending 10 copies of this
letter to the Surface Transportation Board via regular mail this day.
