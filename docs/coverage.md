# Coverage and limitations

> **Status: stub.** Published page. Write before launch — writing it forces you to measure your gaps rather than assume them.

## Purpose

Exactly what is in the corpus, what is not, and where quality degrades.

## What this locks in

Nothing technical. It buys the credibility that makes everything else usable.

## Skeleton

- **Date range by record type**, stated separately — decisions, filings, and environmental
  comments each begin at different points.
- **Known holes:** documents with no attachment, non-PDF attachments, scanned material,
  anything the agency itself flags as not searchable.
- **Refresh cadence** and the honest lag between an STB posting and the index.
- **Counts generated automatically** from the store, never hand-typed, so the page cannot
  drift from reality.
