The record as data

Bulk access is a promise, not a feature. Everything {{ site_name }} holds about the Board’s proceedings is downloadable as one file, and every docket, filing and decision has a JSON twin at its own address. The data is dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0): use it for anything, no permission or attribution needed. The Board’s own file remains the authority for every record.

JSON

Append .json to a docket, sub-docket, filing or decision address and the same record comes back as data — a sub-docket answers with its family and names what was asked — with the same permanent address and a Cache-Control of thirty minutes. Feeds are at /feed. There are no keys, no accounts and no rate limits; please be reasonable, identify your client in its User-Agent, and prefer the snapshot for anything that walks the whole record.

Every response carries source, licence and generated_at. Field names follow the snapshot’s schema; a field the Board left blank is null, never guessed. Every response carries shape_version; it is raised, and announced here, when a field changes name or meaning.
