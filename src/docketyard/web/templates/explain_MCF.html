What it is

“Motor carrier passenger proceedings.”{{ g('B') }} The Board keeps the ICC’s authority over mergers and control among intercity bus companies (49 U.S.C. §14303) — “the intercity bus industry” is on the Board’s own list of what it regulates.{{ g('B') }}

What is being asked for

Approval, or an exemption, for one bus company to acquire another or to be brought under common control: “Flixbus … and Greyhound Lines — Control — Greyhound Central” (MCF 21155), “First Student — Acquisition of Control” (MCF 21154).{{ g('C') }} The registry’s {{ pf.dockets | commas }} MCF dockets are overwhelmingly ICC-era trucking and bus transactions — the “(T1)/(T2)/(EX-NR)” caption codes are the ICC’s own{{ g('C') }} — of which {{ f.suffix_count('TA', 'MCF') | commas }} carry the TA suffix, which in this series appears to mark the ICC’s temporary authority grants; that reading is unconfirmed.{{ g('?') }} Since 1996 the live MCF work is bus control transactions only.{{ g('C') }}
