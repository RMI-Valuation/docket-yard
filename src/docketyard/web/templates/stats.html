The record in numbers

Every number here is measured from the record as it stands; none is typed. A filing or decision is counted once by the Board’s own identifier, even when it is entered under a parent and a sub-docket, and an environmental comment once by the Board’s number and row — the same units the coverage page reports. The chart below is filings and decisions only; comments arrive on their own walk and are not yet drawn. Months are by the Board’s own dates — filed or served — so a month’s number is what this record holds dated in that month, whichever wave observed it; the earliest months fill in as the backfill reaches them. Nothing on this page is about who reads or follows.
