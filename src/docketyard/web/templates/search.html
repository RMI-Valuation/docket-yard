Search the record

A docket number opens its sheet. Anything else is matched against captions as the Board prints them, filings by their type and who they were filed for, decisions, environmental comments, the names parties are on record under, and the text of documents as read by machine. Results are grouped by proceeding. Nothing you search for is kept.

A proceeding is a docket sheet: a sub-docket with a caption of its own is its own proceeding, as each line abandonment is; one that repeats its parent's caption is part of its parent. A record entered in several proceedings shows under each, and a document filed in several counts in each. Text of documents is what a machine read from the Board's files, labelled with who read it; the scan is the record.
