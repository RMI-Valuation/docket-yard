Coverage and limitations

Every number on this page is measured from the record as it stands, not typed. Last checked {{ cov.last_checked | fmt_when }}.

What is watched

{{ site_name }} asks the Board's record search for new filings and decisions every thirty minutes, across every docket, and has done so continuously since {{ cov.forward_since | fmt_when }}. Environmental comments joined that watch later, and the date above measures the filings and decisions captures only. An entry the Board posts is usually on its sheet within the hour. The record has {{ cov.filings | plural('filing') }}, {{ cov.decisions | plural('decision') }} and {{ cov.comments | plural('environmental comment') }}, and holds {{ cov.documents | plural('document') }} — the Board's own files, kept by their content hash so a replaced file is noticed{% if cov.attachments_unfetched %}; {{ cov.attachments_unfetched | plural('file') }} listed by the Board have not yet been fetched{% endif %}.

The record spans entries the Board dated from {{ cov.record_from | fmt_date }} to {{ cov.record_to | fmt_date }} — what the watch has caught since it began, plus the dated waves described below. Those are the Board's dates, quoted; the watch's own start is the timestamp above.

The docket registry

The registry of proceedings — {{ '{:,}'.format(cov.dockets) }} dockets — was walked in full from the Board's dockets table{% if cov.registry_walked_at %} on {{ cov.registry_walked_at | fmt_date }}{% endif %}, one prefix at a time. Six prefixes the Board's search offers return nothing ({{ cov.empty_prefixes | join(', ') }}); they are listed so that their absence here is not mistaken for a gap.

What is not here

    {% if cov.backfill_from %}
  • History before {{ cov.backfill_from | fmt_date }}. Filings and decisions dated from {{ cov.backfill_from | fmt_date }} onward are being added in dated waves ({{ cov.backfill_filings | plural('filing') }} and {{ cov.backfill_decisions | plural('decision') }} so far — counted once per docket they were entered in, so a filing entered in a docket and its sub-docket counts twice here and once on the statistics page{% if cov.backfill_incomplete %}; months not yet complete: {{ cov.backfill_incomplete | join(', ') }}{% endif %}); before that date a sheet is not the docket's complete history and does not claim to be. Entries added by a wave never trigger an alert.
  • What crawlers are told. The sitemap lists every docket address — families and sub-dockets — every filing and decision, every party page, and every document the record holds at its hash; it is regenerated from the record as it moves.
    • {% else %}
  • History before the watch began. A docket's sheet shows what the Board has posted since {{ cov.forward_since | fmt_date }}, plus anything observed while building the registry. Older filings and decisions are real and will be added in dated waves; until then a sheet is not the docket's complete history and does not claim to be.
    • {% endif %}
  • Rail recordations, which the Board keeps in a separate table.
  • Document text. Files are kept and linked; nothing is yet extracted from inside them.
  • Anything the Board's search itself does not show. Its result tables display at most 10,000 rows per query and fail in ways that look like success; the pipeline asserts on every response that the filter it asked for was applied, and quarantines anything it cannot prove.
