What is watched

{{ site_name }} asks the Board's record search for new filings and decisions every thirty minutes, across every docket, and has done so continuously since {{ cov.forward_since | fmt_when }}. An entry the Board posts is usually on its sheet within the hour. The record has {{ cov.filings | plural('filing') }} and {{ cov.decisions | plural('decision') }}, and holds {{ cov.documents | plural('document') }} — the Board's own files, kept by their content hash so a replaced file is noticed{% if cov.attachments_unfetched %}; {{ cov.attachments_unfetched | plural('file') }} listed by the Board have not yet been fetched{% endif %}.

The earliest filing observed under this watch is dated {{ cov.earliest_filed | fmt_date }}; the earliest decision, {{ cov.earliest_served | fmt_date }}. Those are the Board's dates, quoted.

The docket registry

The registry of proceedings — {{ '{:,}'.format(cov.dockets) }} dockets — was walked in full from the Board's dockets table{% if cov.registry_walked_at %} on {{ cov.registry_walked_at | fmt_date }}{% endif %}, one prefix at a time. Six prefixes the Board's search offers return nothing ({{ cov.empty_prefixes | join(', ') }}); they are listed so that their absence here is not mistaken for a gap.

What is not here

    {% if cov.backfill_from %}
  • History before {{ cov.backfill_from | fmt_date }}. Filings and decisions dated from {{ cov.backfill_from | fmt_date }} onward are being added in dated waves ({{ cov.backfill_filings | plural('filing') }} and {{ cov.backfill_decisions | plural('decision') }} so far{% if cov.backfill_incomplete %}; months not yet complete: {{ cov.backfill_incomplete | join(', ') }}{% endif %}); before that date a sheet is not the docket's complete history and does not claim to be. Entries added by a wave never trigger an alert.
    • {% else %}
  • History before the watch began. A docket's sheet shows what the Board has posted since {{ cov.forward_since | fmt_date }}, plus anything observed while building the registry. Older filings and decisions are real and will be added in dated waves; until then a sheet is not the docket's complete history and does not claim to be.
    • {% endif %}
  • Environmental comments and rail recordations, which the Board keeps in separate tables.
  • Document text. Files are kept and linked; nothing is yet extracted from inside them.
  • Anything the Board's search itself does not show. Its result tables display at most 10,000 rows per query and fail in ways that look like success; the pipeline asserts on every response that the filter it asked for was applied, and quarantines anything it cannot prove.
